# Help: projects, colors and items

A **project** is one request: "match these 25 colors", "find out why this ink is not drying", "trial our new white on their press". The pieces inside it are **items**. On a color match the items are colors; on a customer issue they are the products involved; on an R&D project they are trials.

## Project types

- **Color match**: a customer, one or more colors, codes assigned when matched.
- **Customer issue**: a problem statement, the products involved, a root cause and a resolution. Usually no new codes.
- **Customer evaluation**: a goal, the items under evaluation, and an outcome. May end in new codes.
- **R&D**: internal work with a lead, an optional team, a goal and a stage (idea, bench, scale-up, press trial, released or shelved). Can be marked confidential.

## Items

Each item has its own status (pending, in progress, matched, approved, rejected or not needed), its own tech, dates, proofs, coating, notes and codes. The project's progress bar counts the items that are done. A project can hold up to 50 items.

## Activity, tasks and attachments

- **Activity** is the project's diary: notes you write plus automatic entries like "sell number Y1715UV assigned".
- **Tasks** are next actions with an owner and a due date. Your open tasks are listed on the home page.
- **Attachments** hold drawdown photos, iQC exports, artwork and reports.

## Finishing

Mark each item done as you go. When every item is done (or marked not needed) the project completes itself. You can also complete it by hand from the project page.
