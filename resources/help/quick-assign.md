# Help: Quick assign

**Quick assign** gives out an E number or a sell number **without a project**. Use it for:

- **another sell number for an existing product** — a relabel, a customer-specific code, a new version (for example the UV version of the same formula). This is the usual case.
- **a brand-new product that never goes through the lab** — a resale item, a purchased product, a formula with no project.

Codes for colors inside a project are assigned from the project page instead, so they stay attached to the right color.

## Easy mode: three steps

1. **Which product?** Pick *An existing product* and search by E number, sell number or a word from the description — any of a product's sell numbers finds the same product. Pick *A brand-new product* if nothing has been coded for it yet.
2. **Which code?** Choose what you need (a sell number, an E number, or both), then the prefix — the next free number is shown next to each one, for example *next: Y1715* — and a suffix if the prefix allows one. The description is filled in from the existing product; change it if the new code means something different. Say **why** the product needs another sell number (relabel, customer-specific code, new version, resale, other — for *other*, a few words of your own). The customer and a note are optional.
3. **Check and confirm.** Nothing is reserved until you press **Assign the code**. The success screen shows the new code large, with a **Copy** button, and links to open it or assign another.

## Expert mode: one page

Open **Quick assign** in the top bar. Everything is on one form; the box on the right shows the next free numbers as you choose. Press **Assign the code** and the result appears on the same page.

## What happens behind the scenes

- The new sell number is **linked to the product's E number**, so the lookup shows all of a product's codes together.
- If the product only had a sell number and no E number, choosing *An E number* gives it one and links the existing sell number to it. *Both* does that and adds another sell number in one go.
- A brand-new product can get its E number and its sell number together; they are assigned in one step and linked.
- Chroma always uses the next free number under the prefix. Numbers are never reused: if the code turns out to be a mistake, open it and **void** it with a reason.
- Every assignment is written to the log with your name, the reason and the customer.

## Permissions

You need "Assign sell numbers" to give out sell numbers and "Assign E numbers" to give out E numbers. If an option is missing from the form, ask an administrator for the permission.
